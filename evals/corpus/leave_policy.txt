Leave policy. Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.
Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Parental leave is 16 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the other parent.
Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive days need approval from the department head at least 30 days in advance.
