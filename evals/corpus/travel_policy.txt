Travel policy. Business class flights are allowed only for trips longer than 8 hours and need written manager approval. Economy class is the default for all other trips.
Hotel stays are capped at 150 dollars per night in standard cities and 220 dollars per night in high-cost cities such as London and New York.
Car rentals require approval from the department head and must be compact or mid-size vehicles.

Expense policy. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 40 dollars per day with receipts. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Claims above 500 dollars need a second approval from finance.
